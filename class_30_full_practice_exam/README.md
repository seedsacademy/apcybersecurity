## Composite Score Calculation

$$\text{Composite Score} = \left(\frac{\text{MCQ Correct}}{60} \times 70\right) + \left(\frac{\text{FRQ Points}}{6} \times 30\right)$$

| Projected AP Score | Estimated Composite Score Range | Mastery Description |
| :---: | :---: | :--- |
| **5** | **75 – 100** | Extremely well qualified |
| **4** | **62 – 74** | Well qualified |
| **3** | **50 – 61** | Qualified (Eligible for college credit/placement) |
| **2** | **38 – 49** | Possibly qualified |
| **1** | **0 – 37** | No recommendation |

### Student Activity: Personal Error Taxonomy
After completing and scoring your practice exam:
1. Categorize every missed question by Unit (Unit 1, 2, 3, 4, or 5).
2. Identify whether mistakes were caused by:
   - **Content Knowledge Gap** (Did not know the term/concept)
   - **Evidence Misinterpretation** (Read the log/firewall rule too quickly)
   - **Time Management** (Ran out of time on the section)
3. Build a 2-week targeted study checklist focusing on your weakest unit!
