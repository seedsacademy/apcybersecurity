## 🏆 AP Exam Practice: Free-Response Evidence Prompt (10 Minutes)

**Your Task:** Write an evidence-based response answering all three parts:

1. **Identify the specific social engineering attack and psychological levers** used in this message. Support your identification with direct evidence from the scenario.
2. **Explain the technical consequence** if Marcus downloads and runs `Update_HIPAA_Patch.exe`.
3. **Propose an organizational policy or technical control** that would protect the clinic even if Marcus accidentally clicks the link.
