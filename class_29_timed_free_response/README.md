# Class 29: Timed Free-Response Practice — 50-Minute Simulation

[⬅ Back to Course Roadmap](../README.md) | [📄 Practice FRQ Packet](practice_frq.md) | [📊 Scoring Rubric](scoring_rubric.md)

> [!NOTE]
> **Mission Briefing:** This class is a full-dress rehearsal for Section II of the AP Cybersecurity exam. You will have exactly **50 minutes** to examine an evidence packet containing four device sources, write your complete analysis, and evaluate your work using the official 6-point College Board scoring rubric!

---

## Course Alignment & AP Framework

- **Course:** AP Cybersecurity
- **Exam Target:** **Section II: Free-Response Question (50 Minutes, 30% Weight, 6 Points Total)**
- **Suggested Time Management:**
  - `00–10 min`: Read scenario, cross-reference policy with logs, mark up firewall rules and permissions.
  - `10–25 min`: Draft Task 1 (Policy & Incident Hypothesis) and Task 2 (Vulnerability Analysis).
  - `25–40 min`: Draft Task 3 (Mitigations & Operational Tradeoffs).
  - `40–50 min`: Proofread, ensure specific evidence (timestamps, Event IDs, file paths) is cited.

---

## Lab Instructions

1. Open [practice_frq.md](practice_frq.md).
2. Set a 50-minute timer.
3. Write your responses in a document or on paper without looking at the scoring rubric.
4. When time expires, open [scoring_rubric.md](scoring_rubric.md) and conduct a peer or self-grading review to calculate your score out of 6 points.
